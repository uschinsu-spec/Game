Selected CC0 GLB assets:

- tree_oak.glb
- tree_pine.glb
- tree_palm.glb
- rock_large.glb
- rock_medium.glb
- rock_small.glb
- fence_gate.glb
- fence_simple.glb
- sign.glb

The GLB files use embedded materials and are deliberately lightweight for portrait mobile rendering. The surrounding xianxia architecture, terrain layering, lakes, bridge, lanterns, distant mountains and mist are generated locally by `enhanced-world.js` so the game does not depend on mismatched third-party architecture packs.
